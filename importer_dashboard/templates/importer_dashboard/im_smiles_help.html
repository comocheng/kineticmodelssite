What SMILES.txt looks like 
N2	N#N
AR	[Ar]
CH4	C
C2H3	[CH]=C
CH2(S)	singlet[CH2]	! singlet methylene: [CH2] alone is the triplet
OHV	excited[OH]	! OH(A), the excited state
CH3CF	singletC[C]F
  • One species a line: its name in the mechanism's SPECIES block, a tab or spaces, then its SMILES.
  • Text after ! is a comment. You don't write who identified a species: the Importer records that itself.
  • When the SMILES alone gives the wrong spin, write the spin in front of it: singlet, doublet or triplet, as in singletC[C]F.
  • A few molecules have special names: singlet[CH2], triplet[CH2], singletC=[C], [C], [O]singlet, excited[OH], excited[CH] and others in importChemkin.py.
  • The file can be left out: the Importer identifies small molecules by their formula, and you confirm the others on the Importer page.
